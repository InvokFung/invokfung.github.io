11:T2040,<p>So far each shot has had one or two balls. Real frames have many, and balls meet again on the way. A <strong>kiss</strong> is a second, unplanned contact; a <strong>cannon</strong> is the white bouncing off another ball, on purpose or not. This lesson shows the most common kiss, two ways round it, and how to pot a red and open the pack in one shot.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Series map. <em>★ Basics:</em> 00 Aiming → 01 The hit → 02 Stop, follow, screw → 03 Where the white goes · <em>★★ Club:</em> 04 Thin cuts → 05 Side spin → 06 Cut and side into pockets → 07 Cushions → 08 Doubles → 09 Escaping a snooker · <em>★★★ Advanced:</em> 10 Plants and sets → <strong>11 Kisses and the pack</strong> (this post) → 12 Safety → 13 Position · <em>★★★★ Hard:</em> 14 Two- and three-cushion escapes → 15 Swerve and special spots.</p>
</blockquote>

<h1 id="Learning-Outcome"><a href="#Learning-Outcome" class="headerlink" title="Learning Outcome"></a>Learning Outcome</h1>
<ul>
<li>Spot a <strong>double kiss</strong> before you play.</li>
<li>Get round it by changing the angle, not the pace.</li>
<li>Pot a red and use the white to <strong>open the pack</strong> a little.</li>
<li>Check the white's whole path for other balls.</li>
</ul>

<h1 id="1-The-double-kiss"><a href="#1-The-double-kiss" class="headerlink" title="1 · The double kiss"></a>1 · The double kiss</h1>
<div class="bclip"><img class="no-lightbox" src="/blog/assets/billiards/b11_clip_kiss.webp" alt="Slow-motion clip, close to the top side cushion: the white hits a red that sits 6 cm off the cushion, full ball. The red hits the cushion, comes straight back and hits the white again, and both balls go off line"></div>
<p>A red sits 6 cm off the cushion. You want to double it (post 08). If the white hits it <strong>full</strong>, the red reaches the cushion, bounces straight back, and meets the white again before the white has gone anywhere. That second contact is a <strong>double kiss</strong>: the red goes off line.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Watch out:</strong> pace does not fix it. At power 3, 4 and 5 the white met the red twice every time. The white is simply too close.</p>
</blockquote>

<p><img src="/blog/assets/billiards/b11_kiss.webp" alt="Three top views of the same red near the top side cushion. 1: full ball at power 4: the white and red meet twice, cross. 2: the white comes from 30 degrees to the right at power 5: one contact, the red doubles into the bottom middle pocket, tick. 3: the white comes from 30 degrees to the left at power 5: one contact, the red goes in, tick"></p>

<p>The same kiss happens on a plant or a pot when a ball is close to a cushion and you hit it nearly full <strong>into</strong> the cushion. Before any shot like that, ask: &quot;after the first contact, does the white get out of the way?&quot;</p>

<h1 id="3-Pot-a-red-and-open-the-pack"><a href="#3-Pot-a-red-and-open-the-pack" class="headerlink" title="3 · Pot a red and open the pack"></a>3 · Pot a red and open the pack</h1>
<p><img src="/blog/assets/billiards/b11_pack.webp" alt="Two top views near the pack of reds. Before: a loose red above the pack, the white below and left, a line from the red to the top corner pocket; the tip spot is above centre, power 4. After: the red is potted; the white went off the side cushion and into the top of the pack, and four reds have rolled out"></p>
<p>Early in a frame the reds sit in a pack. You need them <strong>open</strong>, but not spread all over the table. One shot can do both: pot a loose red, and let the white run on into the pack.</p>
<div class="bclip"><img class="no-lightbox" src="/blog/assets/billiards/b11_clip_pack.webp" alt="Clip from the baulk side: the white pots a red near the pack into the top corner with follow, comes off the side cushion and runs into the side of the pack, which opens and lets a few reds out"></div>
<p>Here: a cut of about 20°, top spin, power 4. The red goes in ✓, the white comes off the side cushion and runs into the pack, and <strong>four reds</strong> come out by more than 6 cm. The pack also stops the white: it ends near the pink, not somewhere loose.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Trick:</strong> plan which part of the pack the white meets. A glancing hit on its <strong>side or shoulder</strong> opens a few reds and keeps the white near them; a hard, full hit spreads reds everywhere, some of them safe for nobody.</p>
</blockquote>
