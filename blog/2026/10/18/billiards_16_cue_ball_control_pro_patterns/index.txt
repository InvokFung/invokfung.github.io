<h1 id="Learning-Outcome"><a href="#Learning-Outcome" class="headerlink" title="Learning Outcome"></a>Learning Outcome</h1>
<ul>
<li>Read one pot as a <strong>map</strong> of where the white can finish.</li>
<li>Know which choices still <strong>pot</strong> the ball, and which leave it short.</li>
<li>Set the <strong>pace first</strong>, then the tip.</li>
<li>Pick the <strong>zone</strong>, then read the map backwards for the shot.</li>
<li>Open the reds <strong>off the blue</strong>, and choose the <strong>key ball</strong> before the last red.</li>
<li>Plan the six colours as <strong>one chain</strong> of short, easy moves.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="1-The-position-map"><a href="#1-The-position-map" class="headerlink" title="1 · The position map"></a>1 · The position map</h2>
<p><img src="/blog/assets/billiards/s16_map.webp" alt="Top view of the black end. A red 58 cm from the top right corner pocket, the white 55 cm away at a 30 degree cut. </blockquote>

<h2 id="4-Pick-the-zone-then-read-the-map-backwards"><a href="#4-Pick-the-zone-then-read-the-map-backwards" class="headerlink" title="4 · Pick the zone, then read the map backwards"></a>4 · Pick the zone, then read the map backwards</h2>
<p>Start from the <strong>next</strong> ball, not from this one:</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Next ball</th>
<th>Zone (section 1 map)</th>
<th>The shot</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>the black, into a top corner</td>
<td>near the black spot, a little off it</td>
<td><strong>roll, power 2</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>the black, from below</td>
<td>27 cm below the black spot</td>
<td><strong>stun, power 3</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>a red near the pink</td>
<td>below the pink</td>
<td><strong>roll, power 3</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>a ball on the bottom side cushion</td>
<td>15 cm off it, level with the black</td>
<td><strong>screw, power 3</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>the blue</td>
<td>towards the bottom middle pocket</td>
<td><strong>follow, power 3</strong>, watch the in-off</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>The pro habit: <strong>zone → ring on the map → tip and power.</strong> If two choices reach the zone, take the one with the softer pace and the plainer tip.</p>
<hr>
<h1 id="Chapter-2-—-The-reds"><a href="#Chapter-2-—-The-reds" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 2 — The reds"></a>Chapter 2 — The reds</h1>
<h2 id="5-Into-the-reds-off-the-blue"><a href="#5-Into-the-reds-off-the-blue" class="headerlink" title="5 · Into the reds off the blue"></a>5 · Into the reds off the blue</h2>
<p><img src="/blog/assets/billiards/s16_pack.webp" alt="Two top views of the same blue. The white is below and left of the blue, which is potted into the top middle pocket at a 55 degree cut; the reds sit in their triangle behind the pink. Left, cross: stun at power 3, the white runs along the tangent line into the pink first. Right, tick: screw at power 4, the white bends below that line and runs into the side of the reds, then off two cushions to the top end"></p>
<p>Early in a frame the reds are still in a bunch behind the pink. A good way in is <strong>off the blue</strong> (coaching advice): pot the blue and let the white carry on into the reds. Here the blue is a 55° cut into the top middle pocket. Two choices (model):</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Choice</th>
<th>The white's next ball</th>
<th>Reds moved more than 30 cm</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>stun, power 3</td>
<td>the pink ✗</td>
<td>1</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>screw, power 4</td>
<td>a red ✓</td>
<td>2</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>Stun sends the white along the tangent line, straight into the pink: a kiss you cannot plan. <strong>Screw bends the path behind that line</strong>, and the extra pace carries the white into the side of the reds.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Watch out:</strong> opening the reds is not a pot. Plan where the white ends too: here it runs on to the top end, near the black.</p>
</blockquote>
<hr>
<h2 id="6-The-key-ball"><a href="#6-The-key-ball" class="headerlink" title="6 · The key ball"></a>6 · The key ball</h2>
<p><img src="/blog/assets/billiards/s16_key.webp" alt="Two full-table top views. The last red is gone and the white sits just above and right of the blue. Top, tick: the blue into the bottom middle pocket with stun at power 3; the white runs back to near the baulk cushion, with a short, easy line to the yellow. Bottom, cross: the pink into the bottom right corner with roll at power 2; the white ends at the top end, far from the yellow"></p>
<p>The <strong>key ball</strong> is the colour you take with the <strong>last red</strong>, chosen so the white lands for the yellow (coaching advice). Here the last red is gone and the white sits just above the blue. What each colour leaves (model):</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Key ball</th>
<th>Pots?</th>
<th>The white ends</th>
<th>The yellow next</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>blue</strong>, bottom middle, power 3 stun</td>
<td>yes</td>
<td>near the baulk cushion, 65 cm from the yellow</td>
<td><strong>easy</strong>: 13° into the bottom middle ✓</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>pink</td>
<td>yes, 10 ways in the model</td>
<td>power 2 roll: at the top end, 268 cm away</td>
<td>not easy from any of the 10 ✗</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>black</td>
<td>no pocket at a cut of 45° or less</td>
<td>–</td>
<td>–</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>&quot;Easy&quot; here means: the white 25–120 cm from the yellow, a cut of 8–35°, the yellow within 150 cm of the pocket, and both lines clear.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Trick:</strong> choose the key ball <strong>before</strong> you pot the last red, then play the last red to land on it.</p>
</blockquote>

<h1 id="Chapter-3-—-The-colours-as-one-chain"><a href="#Chapter-3-—-The-colours-as-one-chain" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 3 — The colours as one chain"></a>Chapter 3 — The colours as one chain</h1>
<h2 id="7-Plan-all-six-first"><a href="#7-Plan-all-six-first" class="headerlink" title="7 · Plan all six first"></a>7 · Plan all six first</h2>

<h2 id="8-Shot-by-shot"><a href="#8-Shot-by-shot" class="headerlink" title="8 · Shot by shot"></a>8 · Shot by shot</h2>
<p><img src="/blog/assets/billiards/s16_clear.webp" alt="Six top views, one per colour. Each shows the pot as a yellow dashed line to the pocket, the white's path as a white dashed arrow, a green ring where it stops, and a thin line to the next colour. 1 yellow, power 3 roll, green next. 2 green, power 3 roll, brown next. 3 brown, power 3 stun, blue next. 4 blue, power 2 follow, pink next. 5 pink, power 3 roll, black next. 6 black, power 2 roll"></p>
<p>Look at what the chain does <strong>not</strong> use: no power 4 or 5, no hard screw, no side. Every next cut is between 17° and 26°: an angle to work with, never straight and never thin.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Trick:</strong> an angle of about 15–30° on the next ball is what lets the next shot move the white (post 12, section 3).</p>
</blockquote>
<hr>
<h2 id="9-The-long-move-the-blue"><a href="#9-The-long-move-the-blue" class="headerlink" title="9 · The long move: the blue"></a>9 · The long move: the blue</h2>
<p>The biggest move in the chain is <strong>193 cm</strong>, off the blue (shot 4). It is still a <strong>power 2</strong> shot: follow does the work and the white runs with the natural angle up the table. The long move is played with the softest pace that gets there, so the error stays small.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Common pitfall:</strong> hitting the blue hard &quot;to get up the table&quot;. The extra pace spreads the stopping point (section 3), and the pink cut becomes a guess.</p>
</blockquote>

<h2 id="10-When-position-goes-wrong"><a href="#10-When-position-goes-wrong" class="headerlink" title="10 · When position goes wrong"></a>10 · When position goes wrong</h2>
<p>Even good players miss the zone. Then (coaching advice):</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>You are left with</th>
<th>Do this</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>a pot, but no shot onto the next ball</td>
<td>look for <strong>another pocket</strong> for this ball first</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>a long or thin pot</td>
<td>play the pot <strong>with a safety in mind</strong>: pace that leaves the white safe if it misses (post 11)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>no fair pot</td>
<td>play the <strong>safety</strong> now, not the gamble</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>a ball the plan skipped</td>
<td>re-plan from here: three balls ahead again</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
