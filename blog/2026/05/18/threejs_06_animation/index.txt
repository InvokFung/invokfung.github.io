<h1 id="Chapter-1-—-Delta-time-fundamentals"><a href="#Chapter-1-—-Delta-time-fundamentals" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 1 — Delta-time fundamentals"></a>Chapter 1 — Delta-time fundamentals</h1>
<p>The animation loop runs at the browser's frame rate — usually 60 Hz, sometimes 120 Hz on modern displays, sometimes 30 Hz on a struggling phone. Animation logic must be <strong>frame-rate independent</strong>.</p>

<h2 id="Common-easings"><a href="#Common-easings" class="headerlink" title="Common easings"></a>Common easings</h2>
<p><code>Linear</code>, <code>Quadratic.In/Out/InOut</code>, <code>Cubic.*</code>, <code>Quartic.*</code>, <code>Quintic.*</code>, <code>Sinusoidal.*</code>, <code>Exponential.*</code>, <code>Circular.*</code>, <code>Elastic.*</code>, <code>Back.*</code>, <code>Bounce.*</code>.</p>
<p><code>Quadratic.InOut</code> (s-curve, slow ends, fast middle) is the safe default for UI motion. <code>Cubic.Out</code> (fast start, slow end) is good for &quot;object arrives at destination&quot;.</p>

<h2 id="Costs"><a href="#Costs" class="headerlink" title="Costs"></a>Costs</h2>
<p>Morph targets multiply geometry size by <code>(1 + N)</code> where N = number of targets. A 10k-vertex mesh with 20 expressions = 210k vertices' worth of data. Manageable for a single character; not for crowds.</p>
<h2 id="Use-cases"><a href="#Use-cases" class="headerlink" title="Use cases"></a>Use cases</h2>
<ul>
<li>Lip-sync for talking characters (a basic phoneme set is 8-12 shapes).</li>
<li>Body shape sliders (&quot;character creator&quot;).</li>
<li>Procedural deformation (wave on a flag — though shaders do this better).</li>
</ul>

<h1 id="Chapter-10-—-Performance-tips"><a href="#Chapter-10-—-Performance-tips" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 10 — Performance tips"></a>Chapter 10 — Performance tips</h1>
<ul>
<li><strong>One mixer per character.</strong> Many characters = many mixers + one shared scene; cheap.</li>
<li><strong>Cache <code>clipAction</code></strong> — calling it repeatedly with the same clip returns the same action but allocates lookups.</li>
<li><strong>Pause off-screen characters</strong> — <code>mixer.timeScale = 0</code> while not in frustum.</li>
<li><strong>Skip animations far away</strong> — at distance, snap to a single pose; the player won't notice.</li>
<li><strong>For huge crowds</strong>, use <strong>InstancedMesh</strong> (Post 07) with <strong>GPU-driven animation</strong> via a vertex shader sampling a baked animation texture. Three.js doesn't ship this but the pattern is common (see <code>BatchedAnimation</code> / GPU skinning libraries).</li>
<li><strong>Cull unused bones</strong> — characters often ship with bones for hair / cloth that aren't used; remove in the exporter.</li>
</ul>

<p>Tween a cube from <code>(0, 0, 0)</code> to <code>(5, 2, 0)</code> over 2 s with <code>Cubic.Out</code> easing. On complete, tween back.</p>
<h2 id="Exercise-3-—-Play-a-glTF-animation"><a href="#Exercise-3-—-Play-a-glTF-animation" class="headerlink" title="Exercise 3 — Play a glTF animation"></a>Exercise 3 — Play a glTF animation</h2>
<p>Load a Mixamo character (free, <a href="https://www.mixamo.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener external nofollow noreferrer">mixamo.com</a>). Play its &quot;Idle&quot; clip via <code>AnimationMixer</code>.</p>
<h2 id="Exercise-4-—-Cross-fade"><a href="#Exercise-4-—-Cross-fade" class="headerlink" title="Exercise 4 — Cross-fade"></a>Exercise 4 — Cross-fade</h2>
<p>Same character, two clips: Idle + Run. Press W to start <code>crossFadeTo(run)</code>, release to fade back. Use <code>warp: true</code>.</p>
<h2 id="Exercise-5-—-Morph-targets"><a href="#Exercise-5-—-Morph-targets" class="headerlink" title="Exercise 5 — Morph targets"></a>Exercise 5 — Morph targets</h2>
<p>Load any model with morphs (Khronos <code>RobotExpressive</code> is good). Drive the morph weights from sliders (HTML range inputs). Confirm shapes blend.</p>
<h2 id="Exercise-6-—-Skeleton-helper"><a href="#Exercise-6-—-Skeleton-helper" class="headerlink" title="Exercise 6 — Skeleton helper"></a>Exercise 6 — Skeleton helper</h2>
<p>Add <code>SkeletonHelper</code> to your character. Watch bones move while the animation plays.</p>
