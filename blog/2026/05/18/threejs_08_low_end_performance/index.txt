<h1 id="Learning-Outcome"><a href="#Learning-Outcome" class="headerlink" title="Learning Outcome"></a>Learning Outcome</h1>
<ul>
<li>Cap <code>devicePixelRatio</code> correctly (this alone often doubles framerate).</li>
<li>Apply triangle / texture / draw-call budgets for mobile.</li>
<li>Use on-demand rendering when the scene isn't always animating.</li>
<li>Pick cheaper material families and shadow settings on low-end devices.</li>
<li>Diagnose CPU vs GPU bottlenecks systematically.</li>
<li>Detect device capability and adapt at runtime.</li>
</ul>

<h1 id="Chapter-1-—-Pixel-ratio-the-biggest-free-win"><a href="#Chapter-1-—-Pixel-ratio-the-biggest-free-win" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 1 — Pixel ratio (the biggest free win)"></a>Chapter 1 — Pixel ratio (the biggest free win)</h1>
<p>A modern phone may report <code>window.devicePixelRatio = 3</code> — meaning a 400×800 logical viewport renders at <strong>1200×2400 actual pixels</strong>. That's <strong>9× the pixels</strong> of a desktop running the same canvas at DPR=1.</p>
<pre><code class="language-js"><figure class="highlight js"><table><tr><td class="gutter"><pre><span class="line">1</span><br><span class="line">2</span></pre></td><td class="code"><pre><code class="js"><span class="line">renderer.<span class="hljs-title function_">setPixelRatio</span>(<span class="hljs-title class_">Math</span>.<span class="hljs-title function_">min</span>(<span class="hljs-variable language_">window</span>.<span class="hljs-property">devicePixelRatio</span>, <span class="hljs-number">2</span>));</span><br><span class="line"> </span></code></pre></td></tr></table></figure></code></pre>
<p>Capping at 2 is the standard. The visual difference between DPR=2 and DPR=3 is essentially invisible; the performance difference is dramatic.</p>
<p>For aggressive optimisation, cap at 1.5 or 1 on detected low-end devices. Combine with a quality slider in your UI.</p>

<h1 id="Chapter-3-—-On-demand-rendering"><a href="#Chapter-3-—-On-demand-rendering" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 3 — On-demand rendering"></a>Chapter 3 — On-demand rendering</h1>
<p>If the scene doesn't change every frame (static showroom, paused state), don't render every frame.</p>
<pre><code class="language-js"><figure class="highlight js"><table><tr><td class="gutter"><pre><span class="line">1</span><br><span class="line">2</span><br><span class="line">3</span><br><span class="line">4</span><br><span class="line">5</span><br><span class="line">6</span><br><span class="line">7</span><br><span class="line">8</span><br><span class="line">9</span><br><span class="line">10</span></pre></td><td class="code"><pre><code class="js"><span class="line"><span class="hljs-keyword">let</span> needsRender = <span class="hljs-literal">true</span>;</span><br><span class="line">controls.<span class="hljs-title function_">addEventListener</span>(<span class="hljs-string">&#x27;change&#x27;</span>, <span class="hljs-function">() =&gt;</span> { needsRender = <span class="hljs-literal">true</span>; });</span><br><span class="line"> </span><br><span class="line">renderer.<span class="hljs-title function_">setAnimationLoop</span>(<span class="hljs-function">() =&gt;</span> {</span><br><span class="line">  <span class="hljs-keyword">if</span> (needsRender) {</span><br><span class="line">    renderer.<span class="hljs-title function_">render</span>(scene, camera);</span><br><span class="line">    needsRender = <span class="hljs-literal">false</span>;</span><br><span class="line">  }</span><br><span class="line">});</span><br><span class="line"> </span></code></pre></td></tr></table></figure></code></pre>
<p>In R3F: <code>&lt;Canvas frameloop=&quot;demand&quot; /&gt;</code>. The renderer goes idle when nothing's happening, dropping power consumption to near zero — invaluable on mobile (battery, thermal throttling).</p>
<p>For mixed scenes (some always-animating elements, some not), trigger render on relevant property changes only.</p>

<h1 id="Chapter-5-—-Shadow-settings-for-mobile"><a href="#Chapter-5-—-Shadow-settings-for-mobile" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 5 — Shadow settings for mobile"></a>Chapter 5 — Shadow settings for mobile</h1>
<p>From Post 03, shadows are often the most expensive piece. For mobile:</p>
<pre><code class="language-js"><figure class="highlight js"><table><tr><td class="gutter"><pre><span class="line">1</span><br><span class="line">2</span><br><span class="line">3</span><br><span class="line">4</span><br><span class="line">5</span></pre></td><td class="code"><pre><code class="js"><span class="line">renderer.<span class="hljs-property">shadowMap</span>.<span class="hljs-property">enabled</span> = <span class="hljs-literal">true</span>;</span><br><span class="line">renderer.<span class="hljs-property">shadowMap</span>.<span class="hljs-property">type</span> = <span class="hljs-variable constant_">THREE</span>.<span class="hljs-property">PCFShadowMap</span>;     <span class="hljs-comment">// not PCFSoft on mobile</span></span><br><span class="line">sun.<span class="hljs-property">shadow</span>.<span class="hljs-property">mapSize</span>.<span class="hljs-title function_">set</span>(<span class="hljs-number">1024</span>, <span class="hljs-number">1024</span>);               <span class="hljs-comment">// not 2048</span></span><br><span class="line">sun.<span class="hljs-property">shadow</span>.<span class="hljs-property">camera</span>.<span class="hljs-property">far</span> = <span class="hljs-number">30</span>;                       <span class="hljs-comment">// tighter frustum</span></span><br><span class="line"> </span></code></pre></td></tr></table></figure></code></pre>
<p>Or skip real-time shadows entirely and bake:</p>
<ul>
<li>Static scenery → bake AO + lightmap into textures (Post 03/04). Zero per-frame shadow cost.</li>
<li>One key character → real-time shadow only for it.</li>
<li>Use Drei's <code>&lt;AccumulativeShadows&gt;</code> or a <code>ShadowMaterial</code> plane for cheap &quot;contact shadows&quot;.</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h1 id="Chapter-6-—-Texture-budget"><a href="#Chapter-6-—-Texture-budget" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 6 — Texture budget"></a>Chapter 6 — Texture budget</h1>
<p>GPU memory is precious on mobile. Each 1024² uncompressed texture = 4 MB. 30 textures = 120 MB — half the VRAM on many phones.</p>
<p>Discipline:</p>
<ul>
<li>Resize source textures to power-of-two and to the actual on-screen size needed (1024² for hero, 512² for medium, 256² for background props).</li>
<li>Use <strong>KTX2 / Basis Universal</strong> (Post 04) — ~10× GPU memory savings.</li>
<li>Pack channels (ORM in one RGB image — Post 04).</li>
<li>Avoid <code>wrapS/T = ClampToEdge</code> if you can tile — repeating textures need lower resolution.</li>
<li>For decorative repeated detail, use a <strong>trim sheet</strong> (small atlas tiled across many shapes).</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="Detecting-mobile-low-end"><a href="#Detecting-mobile-low-end" class="headerlink" title="Detecting mobile / low-end"></a>Detecting mobile / low-end</h2>
<pre><code class="language-js"><figure class="highlight js"><table><tr><td class="gutter"><pre><span class="line">1</span><br><span class="line">2</span><br><span class="line">3</span></pre></td><td class="code"><pre><code class="js"><span class="line"><span class="hljs-keyword">const</span> isMobile = <span class="hljs-regexp">/Mobi|Android/i</span>.<span class="hljs-title function_">test</span>(navigator.<span class="hljs-property">userAgent</span>);</span><br><span class="line"><span class="hljs-keyword">const</span> lowEnd = navigator.<span class="hljs-property">hardwareConcurrency</span> &lt; <span class="hljs-number">4</span> || (navigator.<span class="hljs-property">deviceMemory</span> ?? <span class="hljs-number">4</span>) &lt; <span class="hljs-number">4</span>;</span><br><span class="line"> </span></code></pre></td></tr></table></figure></code></pre>
<p>Not perfect (web hides specs by design); good enough to make a tier decision at startup.</p>
<hr>
<h1 id="Chapter-7-—-Geometry-budget"><a href="#Chapter-7-—-Geometry-budget" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 7 — Geometry budget"></a>Chapter 7 — Geometry budget</h1>
<p>Triangle count is rarely the bottleneck for modern GPUs; <strong>fill rate</strong> (pixels × shader cost) is. Still, mobile rasterisers slow on huge meshes:</p>
<ul>
<li>High-detail hero models: ~10-30k triangles.</li>
<li>Background props: ≤ 1k each.</li>
<li>Total scene triangles: ≤ 500k on mid-mobile, ≤ 100k on low.</li>
</ul>
<p>Reduce via:</p>
<ul>
<li><code>gltf-transform simplify</code> (CLI).</li>
<li>LOD swaps (Post 07).</li>
<li>Decimate / retopo in Blender.</li>
</ul>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Trick:</strong> check if your model is over-tessellated for its on-screen size. A 50k-tri tree displayed at 30 pixels tall is wasted work; a 500-tri impostor billboard would look identical.</p>
</blockquote>
<hr>
<h1 id="Chapter-8-—-Postprocess-discipline-on-mobile"><a href="#Chapter-8-—-Postprocess-discipline-on-mobile" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 8 — Postprocess discipline on mobile"></a>Chapter 8 — Postprocess discipline on mobile</h1>
<p>Each postprocess pass is a full-screen render at the canvas resolution. On a 1200×2400 phone display (DPR=3 capped to 2 → 800×1600), every pass is <strong>1.28 MP</strong> of fragment work. Three passes triple that.</p>
<p>Mobile rules:</p>
<ul>
<li>0-1 postprocess passes maximum.</li>
<li>Run post passes at <strong>half resolution</strong> where possible (<code>composer.setSize(w/2, h/2)</code>).</li>
<li>Skip bloom + SSAO + DOF together — pick one if any.</li>
<li>Tonemapping in the main render pass is free (<code>renderer.toneMapping = THREE.ACESFilmicToneMapping</code>) — keep that.</li>
</ul>
<p>Post 10 covers postprocess in detail.</p>
<hr>
<h1 id="Chapter-9-—-Render-targets-memory"><a href="#Chapter-9-—-Render-targets-memory" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 9 — Render targets + memory"></a>Chapter 9 — Render targets + memory</h1>
<p>Each render target (custom framebuffer used for post passes, reflections, planar mirrors, refraction) = a full-resolution colour + depth texture. On mobile, two render targets at full resolution might already eat 30 MB.</p>
<p>Tips:</p>
<ul>
<li>Half-resolution where the artifact won't be noticed.</li>
<li>Reuse the same render target across passes (ping-pong) instead of allocating new each frame.</li>
<li>Free render targets you no longer use: <code>rt.dispose()</code>.</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h1 id="Chapter-10-—-CPU-vs-GPU-bottleneck-—-diagnosis"><a href="#Chapter-10-—-CPU-vs-GPU-bottleneck-—-diagnosis" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 10 — CPU vs GPU bottleneck — diagnosis"></a>Chapter 10 — CPU vs GPU bottleneck — diagnosis</h1>
<p>Three classes of bottleneck. Different fixes for each.</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Bottleneck</th>
<th>Symptom</th>
<th>Fix</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>CPU</strong> (JS + draw calls)</td>
<td>high JS time in Performance panel; <code>renderer.info.calls</code> high</td>
<td>reduce draw calls (Post 07); move per-frame work out of JS; throttle updates</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>GPU fragment</strong> (pixel shader / fill rate)</td>
<td>lowering pixel ratio drastically improves; high GPU ms</td>
<td>cheaper materials; smaller postprocess; smaller canvas</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>GPU vertex</strong> (geometry)</td>
<td>many high-tri meshes; OK on small canvas, slow on big</td>
<td>LOD; geometry simplification; instancing</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>Quick test: <strong>halve the canvas size</strong> (<code>renderer.setSize(w/2, h/2)</code>). If FPS doubles or more, you're GPU-bound. If little change, you're CPU-bound.</p>
<p>Quick test 2: <strong>disable shadows + post</strong>. If FPS doubles, those were the cost. Tune them.</p>
<p>Use <strong>stats-gl</strong> (Post 00) — separate CPU ms and GPU ms readouts make this diagnosis straightforward.</p>
<hr>

<p>Detect FPS over a window; downgrade quality automatically when it drops.</p>

<h1 id="Chapter-12-—-Bundle-size-load-time"><a href="#Chapter-12-—-Bundle-size-load-time" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 12 — Bundle size + load time"></a>Chapter 12 — Bundle size + load time</h1>
<p>Slow first paint kills mobile users on cellular. Reduce JS bundle:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Tree-shake</strong> — Vite + ESM does this automatically; avoid importing <code>three</code> namespaces from anything but <code>three/addons/*</code>.</li>
<li><strong>Lazy-load</strong> decoders (Post 05) — DRACOLoader + KTX2Loader pulled only when needed.</li>
<li><strong>Lazy-load</strong> Three.js itself — dynamic <code>import('three')</code> inside the first interaction handler if 3D isn't the main page content.</li>
<li>Show a <strong>loading indicator</strong> during decode (10-100 MB models can take seconds even after download).</li>
</ul>

<h1 id="Chapter-13-—-The-mobile-audit-checklist"><a href="#Chapter-13-—-The-mobile-audit-checklist" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 13 — The mobile audit checklist"></a>Chapter 13 — The mobile audit checklist</h1>
<p>Before shipping any Three.js page to mobile:</p>
<ul>
<li>[ ] <code>renderer.setPixelRatio(Math.min(devicePixelRatio, 2))</code>.</li>
<li>[ ] Draw calls measured + ≤ 100-200.</li>
<li>[ ] Triangles ≤ 500k.</li>
<li>[ ] Textures KTX2-compressed; total ≤ 100 MB GPU.</li>
<li>[ ] Shadows: 1 × 1024 PCF (or baked AO only).</li>
<li>[ ] Postprocess: 0-1 passes max.</li>
<li>[ ] On-demand rendering if scene isn't always animating.</li>
<li>[ ] Tested on the lowest-end target device, not just dev machine.</li>
<li>[ ] Loading indicator + progress.</li>
<li>[ ] Lazy-loaded decoders, lazy-loaded Three.js if 3D is secondary.</li>
<li>[ ] Adaptive quality loop in place.</li>
<li>[ ] Battery + thermal tested for sessions &gt; 1 minute.</li>
</ul>
