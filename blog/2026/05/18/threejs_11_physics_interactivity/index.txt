11:Td79a,<p>Three.js does not include a physics engine — meshes don't collide, fall, or push each other by themselves. For physics + collisions you add a separate library; the modern default in 2026 is <strong>Rapier</strong> (Rust + WASM, fast, deterministic). For interaction (click / drag / hover), Three.js provides <code>Raycaster</code> and several controls (<code>TransformControls</code>, <code>DragControls</code>, <code>PointerLockControls</code>). This post covers wiring physics into a Three.js scene, the standard raycast patterns, and the gizmos you need for editor-like apps.</p>
