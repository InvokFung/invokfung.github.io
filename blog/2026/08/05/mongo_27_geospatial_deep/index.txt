<h1 id="Learning-Outcome"><a href="#Learning-Outcome" class="headerlink" title="Learning Outcome"></a>Learning Outcome</h1>
<ul>
<li>Pick between 2dsphere and legacy 2d indexes.</li>
<li>Store points + polygons + multi-polygons as GeoJSON.</li>
<li>Query with <code>$near</code>, <code>$nearSphere</code>, <code>$geoWithin</code>, <code>$geoIntersects</code>, <code>$centerSphere</code>, <code>$polygon</code>.</li>

<li>Shard by region without breaking geo queries.</li>

<p>Devices write <code>current_location</code> to a per-device document; a change-stream worker (Post 12) does the fence check + emits events. Decouples ingest from geo logic.</p>
<p>For high-volume telemetry (10k devices, 1 Hz each), pattern B is more scalable.</p>
<hr>
<h1 id="Chapter-8-—-Distance-accuracy"><a href="#Chapter-8-—-Distance-accuracy" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 8 — Distance + accuracy"></a>Chapter 8 — Distance + accuracy</h1>
<p>2dsphere uses WGS84 — the standard Earth ellipsoid. Distances are accurate within centimetres of haversine over the surface.</p>
<p>Edge cases:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Polar regions</strong> — geometries cross the pole; spherical math handles them but UIs often need special rendering.</li>
<li><strong>Antimeridian</strong> (180° / -180° longitude in the Pacific) — polygons spanning it must be split. Tooling like <code>@turf/turf</code> (Node) helps.</li>
<li><strong>Long lines</strong> — straight in 2D maps but geodesic in 3D. Use spherical <code>$geoIntersects</code>; map UIs render great-circle correctly.</li>
</ul>
<p>Don't store <code>location</code> as <code>{ lat: ..., lng: ... }</code> if you want geo queries. Always GeoJSON-shaped (<code>{ type: &quot;Point&quot;, coordinates: [lng, lat] }</code>).</p>
<hr>

<h1 id="Chapter-10-—-Sharding-geospatial"><a href="#Chapter-10-—-Sharding-geospatial" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 10 — Sharding + geospatial"></a>Chapter 10 — Sharding + geospatial</h1>
<p><code>2dsphere</code> works on sharded clusters. Shard keys:</p>
<ul>
<li>For multi-region apps, shard on a <strong>region tag</strong> (<code>{ region: 1, _id: 1 }</code>) — keeps each region's data on its own shard, geo queries within a region target one shard.</li>
<li>A <strong>hashed location</strong> shard key spreads writes evenly but scatters geo queries across all shards (bad for hot regions).</li>
</ul>
<p>For dispatch apps where &quot;find nearest courier&quot; frequently hits one region, region-tagged sharding wins.</p>

<h1 id="Chapter-13-—-Common-pitfalls"><a href="#Chapter-13-—-Common-pitfalls" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 13 — Common pitfalls"></a>Chapter 13 — Common pitfalls</h1>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Pitfall</th>
<th>Cause</th>
<th>Fix</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Point in South Atlantic</td>
<td>swapped lat/lng</td>
<td>always <code>[lng, lat]</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>$near</code> returns no results</td>
<td>no 2dsphere index</td>
<td>create the index</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>$near</code> in aggregation <code>$match</code></td>
<td>not allowed</td>
<td>use <code>$geoNear</code> as first stage</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Wrong unit in <code>$centerSphere</code></td>
<td>radius in radians, not metres</td>
<td><code>metres / EARTH_RADIUS_METRES</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Polygon not closed</td>
<td>first ≠ last coord</td>
<td>repeat the first as the last</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Polygon crosses antimeridian</td>
<td>rendered as huge wrap</td>
<td>split at ±180°</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Slow geo queries</td>
<td>low-selectivity index</td>
<td>combine with a <code>query</code> (status, type) filter</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Distances off by huge factor</td>
<td>mixed 2d (planar) + 2dsphere assumptions</td>
<td>always 2dsphere for Earth coords</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Inconsistent precision</td>
<td>storing as floats with 4-decimal rounding</td>
<td>store all 7 decimals for sub-cm</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Sharded geo cross-shard</td>
<td>shard key doesn't include region</td>
<td>shard on region</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h1 id="Practice-Exercises"><a href="#Practice-Exercises" class="headerlink" title="Practice Exercises"></a>Practice Exercises</h1>
<h2 id="Exercise-1-—-Insert-query-points"><a href="#Exercise-1-—-Insert-query-points" class="headerlink" title="Exercise 1 — Insert + query points"></a>Exercise 1 — Insert + query points</h2>
<p>Insert 100 random points around London (lng -0.4 to 0.2, lat 51.3 to 51.6). Index 2dsphere. Find the 10 nearest to King's Cross.</p>
<h2 id="Exercise-2-—-Polygons"><a href="#Exercise-2-—-Polygons" class="headerlink" title="Exercise 2 — Polygons"></a>Exercise 2 — Polygons</h2>

<h2 id="Exercise-3-—-Geofence-pattern"><a href="#Exercise-3-—-Geofence-pattern" class="headerlink" title="Exercise 3 — Geofence pattern"></a>Exercise 3 — Geofence pattern</h2>

<h2 id="Exercise-4-—-geoNear-aggregation"><a href="#Exercise-4-—-geoNear-aggregation" class="headerlink" title="Exercise 4 — $geoNear aggregation"></a>Exercise 4 — <code>$geoNear</code> aggregation</h2>

<h2 id="Exercise-5-—-Range-pagination-by-distance"><a href="#Exercise-5-—-Range-pagination-by-distance" class="headerlink" title="Exercise 5 — Range pagination by distance"></a>Exercise 5 — Range pagination by distance</h2>
<p>Build the cursor-based geo pagination from Chapter 9. Page through 100 places.</p>
<h2 id="Exercise-6-—-Sharded-geo"><a href="#Exercise-6-—-Sharded-geo" class="headerlink" title="Exercise 6 — Sharded geo"></a>Exercise 6 — Sharded geo</h2>
<p>(Atlas / local sharded.) Shard a <code>couriers</code> collection on <code>{ city: 1, _id: 1 }</code>. Confirm <code>$geoNear</code> queries with <code>city: &quot;London&quot;</code> target one shard.</p>

<h1 id="Cheat-sheet"><a href="#Cheat-sheet" class="headerlink" title="Cheat-sheet"></a>Cheat-sheet</h1>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>What</th>
<th>How</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Index</td>
<td><code>{ field: &quot;2dsphere&quot; }</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Point</td>
<td><code>{ type: &quot;Point&quot;, coordinates: [lng, lat] }</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Polygon</td>
<td><code>{ type: &quot;Polygon&quot;, coordinates: [[ [lng,lat], ..., [first lng,lat] ]] }</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Nearest sorted</td>
<td><code>$near</code> (find)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Nearest in aggregation</td>
<td><code>$geoNear</code> (first stage)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Inside polygon</td>
<td><code>$geoWithin: { $geometry: polygon }</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Inside circle</td>
<td><code>$geoWithin: { $centerSphere: [centre, radius/EARTH_R] }</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Intersects</td>
<td><code>$geoIntersects: { $geometry: ... }</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Distance in metres</td>
<td>use 2dsphere; result in metres</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Coordinate order</td>
<td><strong>lng, lat</strong> — not lat, lng</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Polygon closure</td>
<td>first = last coord</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Antimeridian</td>
<td>split polygons crossing ±180</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Geofence</td>
<td>poll <code>$geoIntersects</code> per update or change-stream-driven</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Pagination by distance</td>
<td>cursor on <code>(dist, _id)</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Sharding</td>
<td>region prefix → in-region queries hit one shard</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Helper libs</td>
<td><code>@turf/turf</code>, <code>shapely</code>, <code>H3</code></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
