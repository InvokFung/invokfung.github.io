<h1 id="5-Choosing-a-route"><a href="#5-Choosing-a-route" class="headerlink" title="5 · Choosing a route"></a>5 · Choosing a route</h1>
<p>When more than one route works, choose the one that:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Keeps the white away from pockets and other balls</strong> on the way.</li>
<li>Lands in the <strong>biggest area</strong> that leaves a pot on the next ball (post 13).</li>
<li>Needs the <strong>least side</strong>. No side is the most accurate pot.</li>
</ol>
<p>So if the plain route is good enough, play it. Use side when it opens a clearly better landing spot.</p>
