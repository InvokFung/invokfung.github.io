<h2 id="1-Mirror-the-pocket"><a href="#1-Mirror-the-pocket" class="headerlink" title="1 · Mirror the pocket"></a>1 · Mirror the pocket</h2>
<p><img src="/blog/assets/billiards/b08_mirror.webp" alt="Top view of a red near the top side cushion, a little left of the top middle pocket. A dashed line goes from the red up to a yellow point on the cushion and on towards a mirror pocket above the table. The red's track goes up to that point, comes back down across the table and into the bottom middle pocket, tick. The white sits below the red, on the line"></p>
<ol>
<li>Picture the pocket <strong>reflected</strong> behind the cushion, the same distance on the other side (the mirror pocket).</li>
<li>The red must go <strong>at the mirror pocket</strong>. Where that line meets the cushion is the point the red must hit.</li>
<li>Now it is a normal pot at the mirror pocket: find the ghost ball and aim (post 02).</li>
</ol>
<p>A red that has just been hit is <strong>sliding</strong>, and a sliding ball comes off a cushion close to the mirror line (post 07).</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Trick:</strong> check the line from the other end. Stand behind the pocket and look at the cushion point: the red should sit on the reflected line.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>At high pace the red comes off the cushion a little <strong>short</strong> (squarer). At power 5 the aim needed turning about 0.2° to make up for it. So: play doubles at the <strong>lowest pace that gets there</strong>, and if you must hit hard, aim a touch longer.</p>
<hr>
<h1 id="Chapter-2-—-Cross-cut-back-and-the-kiss"><a href="#Chapter-2-—-Cross-cut-back-and-the-kiss" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 2 — Cross, cut-back and the kiss"></a>Chapter 2 — Cross, cut-back and the kiss</h1>
<h2 id="5-Two-ways-to-play-it"><a href="#5-Two-ways-to-play-it" class="headerlink" title="5 · Two ways to play it"></a>5 · Two ways to play it</h2>
<p><img src="/blog/assets/billiards/b08_two_ways.webp" alt="Two top views of the same double into the bottom middle pocket, both potted. 1: the white comes from the lower left; after contact it runs right, across the red's path back from the cushion. 2: the white comes from the lower right; after contact it goes up and away to the left, not crossing the red's path"></p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th></th>
<th>Cross double (1)</th>
<th>Cut-back double (2)</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>White comes from</td>
<td>the same side the red comes back to</td>
<td>the other side</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>After contact the white</td>
<td>runs <strong>across</strong> the red's path back</td>
<td>goes <strong>away</strong> from it</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Main risk</td>
<td>a <strong>kiss</strong>: the red hits the white on its way back</td>
<td>a thinner hit, so less pace and a harder line</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>Neither is &quot;the&quot; right way; it depends on where the white is.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="6-Watch-it-a-cross-double"><a href="#6-Watch-it-a-cross-double" class="headerlink" title="6 · Watch it: a cross double"></a>6 · Watch it: a cross double</h2>
<div class="bclip"><img class="no-lightbox" src="/blog/assets/billiards/b08_clip_cross.webp" alt="Clip: the red near the top side cushion, the white below and to the left. A yellow dot on the cushion and a line to the bottom middle pocket show the double. The white hits the red firmly, the red comes off the cushion and runs across into the middle pocket, and the white runs away across the table"></div>
<p>Power 4, centre ball. The white runs across the red's path but has already gone past before the red comes back.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="7-The-double-kiss"><a href="#7-The-double-kiss" class="headerlink" title="7 · The double kiss"></a>7 · The double kiss</h2>
<div class="bclip"><img class="no-lightbox" src="/blog/assets/billiards/b11_clip_kiss.webp" alt="Slow-motion clip, close to the top side cushion: the white hits a red that sits 6 cm off the cushion, full ball. The red hits the cushion, comes straight back and hits the white again, and both balls go off line"></div>
<p>A red 6 cm off the cushion, hit <strong>full</strong>: the red reaches the cushion, bounces straight back, and meets the white again before the white has gone anywhere. That second contact is a <strong>double kiss</strong>: the red goes off line.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Watch out:</strong> pace does not fix it. At power 3, 4 and 5 the white met the red twice every time. The white is simply too close.</p>
</blockquote>
<hr>
<h2 id="8-Change-the-angle-to-avoid-the-kiss"><a href="#8-Change-the-angle-to-avoid-the-kiss" class="headerlink" title="8 · Change the angle to avoid the kiss"></a>8 · Change the angle to avoid the kiss</h2>
<p><img src="/blog/assets/billiards/b11_kiss.webp" alt="Three top views of the same red near the top side cushion. 1: full ball at power 4: the white and red meet twice, cross. 2: the white comes from 30 degrees to the right at power 5: one contact, the red doubles into the bottom middle pocket, tick. 3: the white comes from 30 degrees to the left at power 5: one contact, the red goes in, tick"></p>
<p>Change the <strong>angle</strong>, so the white moves out of the red's way after the first contact:</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th></th>
<th>White comes in</th>
<th>Contacts</th>
<th>Result</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>1</td>
<td>straight (full ball)</td>
<td>2</td>
<td>✗ double kiss</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>2</td>
<td>30° from the right (cut-back double)</td>
<td>1</td>
<td>✓</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>3</td>
<td>30° from the left (cross double)</td>
<td>1</td>
<td>✓</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>A cut needs more pace; here power 5. Before any shot into a nearby cushion, ask: &quot;after the first contact, does the white get out of the way?&quot;</p>
<hr>
<h1 id="Chapter-3-—-More-doubles"><a href="#Chapter-3-—-More-doubles" class="headerlink" title="Chapter 3 — More doubles"></a>Chapter 3 — More doubles</h1>
<h2 id="9-A-double-into-the-corner"><a href="#9-A-double-into-the-corner" class="headerlink" title="9 · A double into the corner"></a>9 · A double into the corner</h2>
<p><img src="/blog/assets/billiards/s08_corner.webp" alt="Top view of the right half of the table. A red near the top side cushion is doubled off it, down the table into the bottom-right corner pocket, tick. A yellow dot marks the cushion point; the white comes from below left and stops close to where it hit the red"></p>
<p>The same mirror idea works for a corner: reflect the <strong>corner pocket</strong> behind the cushion and send the red at it. Here a red near the top cushion, power 4, aim turned 0.2° from the mirror line (model).</p>
<div class="bclip"><img class="no-lightbox" src="/blog/assets/billiards/s08_clip_corner.webp" alt="Clip: the red near the top side cushion, the white below it. The mirror line and the cushion point are shown, then the white hits the red, the red comes off the cushion and runs down the length of the table into the bottom-right corner"></div>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Trick:</strong> a corner double comes into the pocket at a slant, so give it the <strong>jaws</strong>, not the cushion. If the line runs close along the end cushion, it has only about 2 cm of room (post 06).</p>
</blockquote>
<hr>
<h2 id="10-When-to-play-a-double"><a href="#10-When-to-play-a-double" class="headerlink" title="10 · When to play a double"></a>10 · When to play a double</h2>
<ul>
<li>When the direct pot is blocked or impossible.</li>
<li>When the double is close to straight (the white more or less behind the red, as in picture 1): those are the easiest.</li>
<li>As a &quot;shot to nothing&quot;: a miss sends the red back across the table, often to a place that is awkward for your opponent. Check where it goes before you play.</li>
</ul>
<p>Before you play it, check the white: where does it go at this pace, and is there a pocket or a kiss on its path?</p>
