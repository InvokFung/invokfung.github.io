Assemble

Up to 42,000 particles fly between scenes: my name in type, a line of copy, and stills from Layerline and Tracewise. Drop a photo on the page and they become you.

Where it came from

The image-to-particles effect people share as "pixels that turn into my photo". The best-known write-up is Interactive Particles with Three.js by Bruno Imbrizi on Codrops. This version is written from scratch in plain WebGL, no three.js.

How it works

  • Each scene is sampled once on the CPU: type is filled with dots, an image becomes a halftone where bright pixels are bigger dots.
  • Every particle keeps where it was and where it is going. The vertex shader flies it there with a swirl that peaks mid-flight, so one draw call moves all of them.
  • The scene sweeps in from the left, and the cursor pushes the dots aside.

Your photo

Drop a photo anywhere, or use the + photo button. It stays in your browser. To make it the default here, save a headshot as lab/assemble/portrait.jpg; a plain background works best.
