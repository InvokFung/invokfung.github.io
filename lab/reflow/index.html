Reflow

A wall of my own project copy that flows around two giant initials and around your cursor. Drag a letter, throw it, and the text parts around it like water.

Where it came from

Pretext by Cheng Lou (2026, MIT), the text layout library behind the viral "text flowing around a moving shape" demos. Vendored unmodified as lab/vendor/pretext.js (1.0.0).

How it works

  • The letters and the cursor are drawn into a mask at a quarter of the resolution.
  • For each line band, the free horizontal spans are read off the mask.
  • Pretext's layoutNextLineRange fits the next line into each span; lines are justified with word spacing so both edges hug the shape.
  • The page never asks the DOM how big anything is. The meter shows the cost per frame.
