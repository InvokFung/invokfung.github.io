Morph

My titles, one after another, with the year rolling beside them. The word turns into the next one instead of being swapped. Pick how: glide, decode or liquid.

Where it came from

  • Split and scramble text reveals are the biggest category in React Bits (about 49k stars, #2 in JS Rising Stars 2025). GSAP made SplitText and ScrambleText free in April 2025.
  • Liquid is the blur and threshold morph from Magic UI's Morphing Text.
  • The year rolls like NumberFlow.

How it works

  • Glide finds the longest run of letters the two titles share, in order, and slides those to their new place. "Engineer" never leaves the screen.
  • Every letter is measured once on a canvas, so nothing waits on the DOM's layout.
  • No libraries. About 200 lines.
