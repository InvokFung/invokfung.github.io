Badge

An ID card on a lanyard, hanging from the top of the page. Grab it and throw it; it swings off the strap. Tap it to flip it over to my contact details. It would close the page where contact() is now.

Where it came from

  • Lanyard from React Bits, a 3D badge on a rope that its author called "the coolest React component you'll see online today". That one uses three.js and a physics engine.
  • The foil is in the spirit of Simon Goellner's pokemon-cards-css.

How it works

  • No libraries. The strap and the card are one verlet chain: fourteen strap links that only pull, and a heavier, rigid last link that is the card.
  • The card is plain HTML, so its text stays sharp and selectable. It is moved with one CSS transform per frame and tilted toward the pointer.
  • The foil is the seven project colours from the site, sliding with the tilt under engraved lines.
